Questions, answered

Anything else? Join the waitlist and we'll be in touch.

What is Hypercard?
A prepaid card made for payments your AI agents make for you. You add money, give each agent its own virtual card, and set rules for what each one may buy.
Is it free?
Yes. There's no fee to get the card and no annual fee.
Is it a credit card?
No. It's prepaid: you add money first, and agents can only spend what's on the card. No credit check, no bill.
Which agents does it work with?
Any agent that can pay online with a card, including Instinct, Muse and Hermes.
Do I need to use an AI agent already?
Yes. Early access is for people who already let an agent shop or book for them.
Can I join from my country?
Join from anywhere. We launch in India first and will tell you when Hypercard reaches your country.
